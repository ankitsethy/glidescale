# Company Profile — Glidescale

## What They Do
Glidescale is an AI-powered enterprise strategy consultancy. They build AI infrastructure and growth systems for founders and executives at growth-stage companies.

## Core Services
- AI infrastructure buildout and architecture
- Growth system design and automation
- Strategic AI roadmapping (3-step frameworks)
- Operational AI deployment

## Target Audience (ICP)
- Founders and C-suite executives at growth-stage companies
- Businesses running Google/Meta ads who feel lead-loss pain
- Companies with inefficient manual processes looking to automate via AI
- Enterprises that want measurable ROI from AI adoption, not just "AI for AI's sake"

## Brand Voice
- **Premium and authoritative** — not mass-market SaaS
- **Direct and outcomes-focused** — leads with results, not features
- **Technical credibility** — speaks the language of infrastructure and systems
- **Boutique feel** — personal, high-touch, not a commodity agency

## Design Language
- Dark navy backgrounds (`base-950` → `base-900`)
- Electric purple accent (`#7C5CFF` / `accent-500`)
- Clean, modern aesthetic with subtle motion
- Framer Motion animations convey precision and craft
- Premium typography: Inter (body) + JetBrains Mono (metrics/labels)

## Key Value Propositions
1. AI-powered strategy generation (live demo via Gemini)
2. Enterprise-grade infrastructure thinking applied to growth
3. Measurable, accountable outcomes — not just consulting decks

## Website Conversion Goal
**Primary CTA**: Book a strategy call.
Every section exists to build credibility toward that single conversion action.

## Conversion Funnel (page sections in order)
1. **Hero** — hook + primary CTA
2. **Logo Cloud** — social proof (client logos)
3. **Authority** — credibility signals
4. **Services** — what they offer
5. **Process** — how it works (reduces friction)
6. **StrategyGenerator** — interactive AI demo (highest engagement section)
7. **Founder** — personal trust/credibility
8. **Metrics** — quantified results
9. **Testimonials** — social proof
10. **CTA** — final conversion push
11. **Footer** — links

## Competitive Positioning
Glidescale competes in the AI consultancy / AI agency space. Differentiation:
- Live AI demo on the site (StrategyGenerator) — rare in this category
- Premium boutique aesthetic vs. typical "AI agency" template sites
- Outcome-first positioning vs. technology-first positioning
